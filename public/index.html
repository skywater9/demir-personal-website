PRE-FLIGHT BRIEFING / CONTROLS

SPACE / ↑ Hold main engine. W / S Adjust throttle.
A / D / ← / → Rotate. I J K L Small world-direction thrusters.
E Attach when bay checks are green. ESC Save and menu.

Land upright on the next pad at ≤ 8 m/s to receive fuel and payload. Near a relay, slow to ≤ 5 m/s and align within ±12°. Your bay is on the rocket’s right side. After delivering, use left RCS (J) to back away from the receiver before accelerating.

Refueling is limited to one grant per route stop. Empty fuel or a high-speed collision ends the run. Leaving the page suspends and saves your flight.
