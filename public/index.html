FLIGHT BRIEFING / CONTROLS

W thrusts. S brakes. Mouse aims and steers. Space shoots. Tab expands the map.

You start with no planets at a protected station. It refuels your ship and gives you one cargo every minute. Press E at your station to open upgrades: R buys BOOSTER and F buys WEAPON, each up to level 10.

Enter a planet shield, aim at the planet and press E, then press E repeatedly to capture. Holding E does not count. Progress slowly decays when idle; each planet level requires two more presses. Destroy every turret first. You can move the cursor away once capture starts; leaving the shield range resets progress. Captured planets produce orbiting cargo. Collect the pods to buy upgrades faster.

Owned planets sell BOOSTER (R), WEAPON (F) and PLANET (T) upgrades, each capped at 10. The home station sells BOOSTER and WEAPON only. Booster upgrades improve low-speed acceleration and fuel efficiency. Weapon levels 3/5/10 unlock twin fire, alternating twin fire and a long-range instant laser. If turrets are destroyed, T becomes REPAIR TURRETS after a five-second cooldown; repair all before upgrading. E leaves a shop.

Gun heat builds during firing and cools automatically. Overheating blocks fire for 2.5 seconds. Hits consume fuel; hard impacts cost more. Stars are lethal. You respawn at your station, drop carried cargo and lose one BOOSTER and WEAPON level on death. Capturing transfers access to the planet cargo without adding it to your ship. Cargo orbiting the black hole is worth five per pod.

This site logs anonymous clicks, choices, game actions, and timing for the owner. No names, passwords, typed text, or aim positions are recorded.
