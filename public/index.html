FLIGHT BRIEFING / CONTROLS

W forward thrust. Mouse/trackpad turns the rocket toward the cursor with gradual rotation. S rapidly brakes along the nose axis; sideways drift remains. Mouse aims, SPACE fires. E attaches/detaches. You cannot shoot while docked.

Click the map or choose a destination. Hack any neutral relay by entering the displayed number. Owned non-home planets release cargo pods from their surfaces into close orbits just beyond the surface; collect them in flight, then land to refuel. Refueling is fast when empty and slower near full. Home refills faster but produces no cargo and has no relay. Follow the flashing arrow to the Moon relay to begin.

Spend cargo at any owned non-home relay to buy its next defense with T or press R to buy the next rocket tier: bullet barrier → turret → outer field. Detaching resets a hack. Turret shots cannot enter the relay refuge, but player shots can. Your home is uncapturable. Shots and fast impacts consume fuel and release white fuel-leak particles; harder impacts cost more fuel. Damage greater than remaining fuel destroys the rocket. Equal damage leaves zero fuel, and another hit at zero is fatal. Star contact remains lethal. Cargo drops on death and you respawn at home.

Zero fuel leaves you coasting. The menu does not pause the galaxy. Refresh/reopen this tab within 30 seconds to recover; longer absences start fresh. Press / for chat and ENTER to send. Capture announcements use your color. Chat messages are not included in analytics. With a multiplayer server configured, all pilots share one galaxy and room chat; otherwise this is local practice. Use made-up labels and do not share personal information.

This site logs anonymous clicks, choices, game actions, and timing for the owner. No names, passwords, typed text, or aim positions are recorded.
