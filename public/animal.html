01 THE STORY

A survivor with
a curious streak.

With rust-red fur, alert ears, and a famously bushy tail, the red fox is easy to recognize. But its real talent is staying one step ahead. Foxes learn quickly, change their routines with the seasons, and find opportunity in all kinds of landscapes.

Mostly active at dusk and through the night, a fox spends its days tucked away in a den or another quiet shelter. When evening comes, it sets off alone to explore its territory and search for a meal.

02 AT HOME

One fox.
Many worlds.

Red foxes are found across much of the Northern Hemisphere. They thrive in woodlands and grasslands, but they can also live near farms and towns, where green spaces and quiet corners offer places to explore.

Each fox makes a home range, marking familiar paths and returning to safe resting spots. A den becomes especially important when cubs arrive in spring.
